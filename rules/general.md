# General editing rules

These apply to any short promo video. They are the reasons behind the tool's defaults.

## Frame
- Vertical is 1080x1920 for Reels, TikTok and Douyin. Feed is 1080x1350. Horizontal is 1920x1080.
- Keep text 180 px from the left and right edges of a 1080-wide frame. The app's like/comment/share column covers the right side.
- Keep important text away from the very top and bottom of a vertical frame; the app draws its own captions and buttons there. Check on a phone.

## Text
- Only use words the owner wrote or approved.
- A typing animation must finish before the cut.
- One idea per card. If a line needs shrinking below about 40 px at 1080 wide, split it.
- Do not print URLs that cannot be tapped. Put the link in the caption or bio and say so.

## Picture
- Open with the strongest image, not a logo or a black title card.
- No frozen or duplicated stretches: run `check.py` and look at the contact sheet.
- Use effects to support a moment (a flash on an impact, shake on an explosion). An effect on every shot reads as noise.

## Sound
- Give each video in a set its own music.
- Put a sound effect where the action is visible; trim the effect so its loudest part lands on the action.
- Lower the music to about half while an effect plays.
- Measuring loudness is not listening. Play the result before publishing.

## Claims
- Only state numbers (time spent, cost, results) the owner has confirmed.
- Do not say something was AI-generated unless it was.
- Check that you have the right to publish the footage, music and fonts you use.
